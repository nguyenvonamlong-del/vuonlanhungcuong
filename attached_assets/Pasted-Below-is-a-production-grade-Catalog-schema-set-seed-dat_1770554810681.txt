I’m giving you:

1. `common.schema.json` (final, corrected)
2. Catalog schemas:

* `catalog.index.schema.json` (one file to validate the whole catalog export)
* `catalog.orchidType.schema.json`
* `catalog.potType.schema.json`
* `catalog.decorationType.schema.json`
* `catalog.shipmentType.schema.json`
* `catalog.promotion.schema.json`

3. `catalog.seed.json` (seed dataset including your 12 Vietnamese orchid names)
